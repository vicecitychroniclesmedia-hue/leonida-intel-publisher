Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Leonida Intel Publisher (the "Tool") handles information. The Tool is a private desktop application used only by the owner of the Leonida Intel YouTube channel to manage that channel.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The Tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using the Tool you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. Information the Tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), the Tool requests these permissions for the owner's own YouTube account only:

Permission (scope)Used for
youtube.uploadUploading the owner's own videos and custom thumbnails.
youtube.force-sslSetting video details (title, description, tags, category, audience and paid-promotion declarations, scheduled publish time) and posting the owner's comment on the owner's own videos.
yt-analytics.readonlyReading the owner's own channel analytics (watch time, views, subscriber count) to track channel growth.

The Tool does not access, collect or process data about any other YouTube user, viewer or commenter.

3. How information is used

  • Video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails created by the owner are sent to YouTube only to publish them on the owner's channel.
  • Channel statistics and analytics are used only to show the owner progress toward YouTube channel milestones.
  • No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than managing the owner's channel.

4. How information is stored

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file that is never uploaded or shared.
  • A local log keeps the IDs and scheduled times of videos the Tool uploaded, so it does not upload the same video twice.
  • Channel statistics retrieved from YouTube are kept locally for at most 30 days and are refreshed or deleted after that.
  • The Tool has no servers, no database and no cloud storage. Nothing is stored anywhere other than the owner's computer and YouTube itself.

5. Sharing

The Tool does not sell, rent, share or transfer any information to third parties. Information is only exchanged between the owner's computer and Google/YouTube through the YouTube API Services.

6. Cookies

The Tool is a desktop application and does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. This website does not use cookies or analytics either.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After access is revoked the Tool can no longer reach the YouTube account until the owner signs in again.

8. Deleting data

To delete all data held by the Tool, revoke access as described above and delete the Tool's local credentials and logs folders. Because no data is stored anywhere else, this permanently removes everything the Tool kept. Requests can also be sent to the contact address below and will be handled within 7 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
